FAQ

Questions? Answered.

Simply click "Book a Stroller," select your mall, pick a date and time slot, choose your stroller type, enter your phone number, and pay. You will receive a booking confirmation with a QR code and a physical ticket at the staffed rental point.

Every stroller is deep-cleaned and sanitized after each return. We use child-safe disinfectants and conduct daily hygiene audits. Child and pet strollers are kept completely separate at all rental points.

The base rental is 1 hour. You can extend your rental up to a maximum of 7 hours total, subject to availability. If you need it longer, you can extend through the booking status page or speak to the staff at the rental point.

We offer a 7-10 minute grace period after your rental hour ends. If you return within this window, no extra charges apply. After the grace period, the next hourly charge applies automatically. For example, if you are 15 minutes late on a Basic stroller, an additional ₹20 will be charged.

Yes, you can rent multiple strollers in a single booking or through separate transactions. Each stroller requires its own ₹100 refundable security deposit. This is perfect if you have more than one child or are visiting with friends.

Every tinywheels rental point inside a mall has 1-2 trained staff members. They help verify your booking via QR scan, hand over the stroller, provide a physical ticket as backup, assist with adjustments, and process returns. They also monitor stroller availability and coordinate cleaning.

No worries. Every booking generates both a digital QR code and a physical ticket that our staff provides at the rental point. You can also give your registered phone number to the staff, and they can look up your booking in the system instantly.

Yes, you can cancel anytime before your pickup time. Full refund if cancelled 2+ hours before. 50% refund if cancelled within 2 hours. No refund for no-shows. The security deposit is always refunded within 24 hours of cancellation or return.

Yes! Pet strollers are available at ₹10/hour extra on any plan. They are completely separate from child strollers to maintain hygiene standards, and are available at all our rental points across Delhi and Bangalore.

All our strollers have GPS tracking enabled, so you can always locate the nearest rental point inside the mall. This also helps our operations team monitor stroller location, ensure none are misplaced, and maintain accurate availability data in real time.
